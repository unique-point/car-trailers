This grouping organises illustrations for enquiry. It does not certify that alternative views show the same physical model or establish saleable product variants. Confirm against the business's catalogue before enabling payments. Product copy distinguishes visual features from certified dimensions, ratings, materials and inclusions.

Corrected generic versions supersede earlier generations. The compact box, black trades trailer and high cage front use the corrected masters, even where an earlier export was uploaded again. The byte and pixel comparison of later uploads identified re-exports of previously generated images. The earlier high-cage export and corrected high-cage front are not two products.

`asset-manifest.json` records original source names, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes. Tools shown in the banner are illustrative until the business confirms inclusion. The banner is concept imagery and does not represent a verified operating premises or manufacturing capability.
